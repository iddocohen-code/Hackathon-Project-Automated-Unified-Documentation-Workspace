Shark Mitigation Procedures

What changed: Emergency Shark Siren button added to the panel. See What's New →

When a shark is sighted in or near a monitored zone, lifeguards must act immediately to protect swimmers and surfers. The steps below are the approved emergency response.

Emergency response steps

  1. 1Sound the Emergency Shark Siren — press the red Emergency Shark Siren button on the Shark Mitigation panel to broadcast the evacuation alarm across all zone speakers.
  2. 2Raise the red hazard flag at the lifeguard tower.
  3. 3Clear all swimmers and surfers from the water.
  4. 4Notify Lifeguard Command and log the incident.
