# Storm Surge Response

This document describes the initial response protocol when storm surge conditions are detected in the monitored coastal zone.

## Step 1: Verify meteorological data

Cross-reference the surge prediction from the Surf Console telemetry dashboard against the national weather service forecast. A surge alert is valid when predicted water height exceeds the baseline by 0.5 metres or more.

## Step 2: Issue an advisory

Broadcast a storm surge advisory via the PA system and update the digital signage at all beach access points. Notify the regional emergency management coordinator by phone.

## Step 3: Coordinate evacuation zones

Work with beach marshals to direct people away from low-lying areas within the defined surge zones. Refer to the posted zone maps at each lifeguard tower.

## Step 4: Log and monitor

Record all actions taken, timestamps, and sensor readings in the Surf Console incident log. Continue monitoring until surge conditions subside and an all-clear is issued by the duty supervisor.
