# Shark Mitigation Guide

This guide explains how to respond to shark incidents using the Surf Console's automated safety systems.

## Step 1: Detect the Shark

When a shark is detected within the safety perimeter, the system automatically alerts the lifeguard station. The detection panel shows the shark's position and estimated size on the map overlay.

## Step 2: Trigger the Emergency Shark Siren

To warn swimmers immediately, activate the Emergency Shark Siren from the Shark Mitigation Panel:

1. Open the **Shark Mitigation Panel** in the Surf Console sidebar.
2. Click the **Trigger Siren** button (red, top-right of the panel).
3. The Emergency Shark Siren will sound for 60 seconds and strobe the beach warning lights.
4. Confirm activation — the button turns green and shows "Siren Active".

The siren integration is fully automated: once you click Trigger Siren, the system broadcasts to all connected beach speakers and light arrays without any additional steps.

## Step 3: Clear the Water

After the siren activates, use the PA broadcast feature to instruct swimmers to exit the water. The system logs the incident timestamp and siren duration for reporting purposes.

## Step 4: File an Incident Report

Once the water is clear, navigate to **Reports > Shark Incidents** and submit the automated pre-filled report. The system captures siren activation time, detection coordinates, and lifeguard response time automatically.
