# Ocean Safety Basics

General guidelines for maintaining safety at surf beaches managed through the Surf Console.

## Weather Monitoring

Check the integrated weather dashboard daily. Wind speed above 25 knots triggers a caution flag automatically.

## Flag System

The Surf Console manages the beach flag system:

- **Green flag**: Safe conditions, swimming permitted.
- **Yellow flag**: Moderate hazard, swim with caution.
- **Red flag**: Dangerous conditions, no swimming.

Flags are updated automatically based on sensor data but can be overridden manually by the lifeguard on duty.

## Emergency Contacts

All emergency contact numbers are stored in the **Settings > Emergency Contacts** panel. Ensure contacts are reviewed monthly.
